## Planned, in order

1. **Vessels**: lathed glass vessels with liquid, from any silhouette curve (three-low-poly `vesselProfiles`).
2. **Timber**: Weathered Plank and Hewn Timber with lumber warps and grain (three-low-poly timber).
3. **Ironwork**: picket, post, panel, scrolls, twists, fleur-de-lis, ivy and the overthrow (three-low-poly
   fence geometry, plus the website's graveyard and windowsill ironwork).
4. **Masonry**: the brick pier with alternating quoins (website graveyard).
5. **Fences**: Path Stations, the rustic fence, the iron fence and the gateway.
6. **Windows, Molding, Hardwood Floor**: modernized from the prototype.

Origins:
- **[three-low-poly](https://github.com/jasonsturges/three-low-poly):** the author's Three.js geometry library.
- **The author's website:** Three.js scenes and lab experiments that incubate many of these designs.

GeoNodes Lab overlaps both, and deviates wherever Blender can do better.
